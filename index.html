Rules that keep this working

  • Keep JUNIORBEES at or below half of NIFTYBEES. More than that and your "large-cap core" stops being the core.
  • MON100: if its price is more than ~2% above iNAV on SIP day, put that month's MON100 money into NIFTYBEES.
  • Never stop the SIP in a fall. Falls are when the SIP buys the most units for the same rupees.
  • Once a year (April): look at what you actually hold. Send new money to whatever is below target, so you don't have to sell.
