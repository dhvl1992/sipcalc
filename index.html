Rules that keep this working

  • Keep JUNIORBEES at or below half of NIFTYBEES. More than that and your "large-cap core" stops being the core.
  • MON100: if its price is more than ~2% above iNAV on SIP day, put that month's MON100 money into NIFTYBEES.
  • Never stop the SIP in a fall. Falls are when the SIP buys the most units for the same rupees.
  • Use "Fill the gaps". New money goes to whatever is below target, so you rebalance without selling (no tax).

How this works

  • Each future is built from real past days since 2011, picked in 5-day blocks so crash days stay together.
  • It shows a range, not a forecast. Nobody knows which way the market goes next.
  • Real crashes can be worse than anything in the data.
